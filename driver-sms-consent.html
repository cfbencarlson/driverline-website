Driver SMS Consent

Public documentation of how active DriverLine delivery drivers opt in to operational SMS messages during onboarding and contractor setup.

Overview

This page documents DriverLine's driver SMS opt-in process so carriers, compliance reviewers, and drivers can understand how DriverLine collects SMS consent.

DriverLine SMS is used only for operational communication with active DriverLine delivery drivers. It is not used for customers, prospects, website visitors, marketing leads, promotions, or sales.

How Drivers Opt In

  1. A driver is onboarded by DriverLine or completes contractor setup.
  2. DriverLine collects the driver's mobile phone number directly from the driver.
  3. Before any SMS messages are sent, the driver is shown the SMS consent language below.
  4. The driver opts in by checking an unchecked opt-in box or signing onboarding paperwork acknowledging SMS consent.
  5. SMS consent is optional. Drivers who decline SMS may coordinate with DriverLine through other available communication methods.

Exact SMS Consent Language

The following exact language is shown to drivers before they opt in:

“By providing your mobile number and opting in, you agree to receive operational SMS messages from DriverLine related to route confirmations, scheduling, dispatch updates, availability checks, and delivery coverage coordination. Message frequency varies based on assigned routes and operational needs. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. SMS consent is optional and is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.”

Program Details

Program name

DriverLine Operations SMS

Message type

Operational route confirmation and dispatch communication for active DriverLine drivers.

Message frequency

Varies based on assigned routes and operational needs.

Opt-out

Drivers can reply STOP at any time to any DriverLine message to opt out. A single confirmation message is sent after opt-out, and no further messages are sent unless the driver re-enrolls.

Help

Drivers can reply HELP to any DriverLine message, or contact DriverLine at info@yourdriverline.com or 720-780-5705.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply depending on the driver's mobile carrier and plan. DriverLine does not charge for SMS messages.

Data sharing

SMS opt-in consent and mobile phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
