For SMS Aggregator / TCR Reviewers

  • DriverLine SMS is only for active DriverLine delivery drivers.
  • There is no public SMS signup form because website visitors, customers, prospects, and marketing leads are not eligible for this SMS program.
  • SMS consent is collected during DriverLine driver onboarding or contractor setup.
  • This page and the linked PDF are hosted proof of that offline consent workflow.
  • The campaign is operational only: route confirmations, scheduling coordination, dispatch updates, availability checks, and same-day delivery coverage communication.

Step-by-Step Offline Consent Workflow

  1. Driver completes DriverLine onboarding or contractor setup.
  2. Driver provides mobile number directly to DriverLine.
  3. Driver reviews the SMS consent section in onboarding paperwork.
  4. Driver checks an unchecked SMS opt-in checkbox or signs paperwork containing equivalent SMS consent language.
  5. SMS consent is optional. Drivers who decline SMS may coordinate with DriverLine through other available communication methods.
  6. DriverLine enables SMS only after consent is on file.
  7. Driver may reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time.

Exact SMS Consent Language

The following exact language appears in the onboarding consent section that each contracted driver reviews before SMS messages are sent:

“By providing your mobile number and opting in, you agree to receive operational SMS messages from DriverLine related to route confirmations, scheduling coordination, dispatch updates, availability checks, and same-day delivery coverage communication. Message frequency varies based on assigned routes and operational needs. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. SMS consent is optional and is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.”

Program Details

Program name

DriverLine Operations SMS

Message type

Operational route confirmation and dispatch communication for active DriverLine drivers.

Message frequency

Varies based on assigned routes and operational needs.

Opt-out

Drivers can reply STOP at any time to any DriverLine message to opt out. A single confirmation message is sent after opt-out, and no further messages are sent unless the driver re-enrolls.

Help

Drivers can reply HELP to any DriverLine message, or contact DriverLine at info@yourdriverline.com or 720-780-5705.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply depending on the driver's mobile carrier and plan. DriverLine does not charge for SMS messages.

Data sharing

SMS opt-in consent and mobile phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
