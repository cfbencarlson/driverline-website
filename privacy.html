Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

DriverLine is a service operated by ATOR Enterprises Inc. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: October 2026

Privacy Policy

Who We Are

DriverLine is a delivery driver solutions service operated by ATOR Enterprises Inc. DriverLine provides managed delivery driver services to businesses with recurring delivery routes.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and business name when you request coverage or contact us — including phone numbers provided by customers, prospects, drivers, or contractor applicants.
  • Business Information: Details about your delivery needs, locations, and operational requirements.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited and time spent.
  • Communication Records: Records of emails, calls, and messages exchanged with our team, including SMS communication records where applicable.
  • SMS Consent Records: Records of SMS opt-in consent and opt-out requests, where SMS communications are used.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Evaluate your delivery coverage needs and provide service quotes
  • Communicate with you about inquiries, service coordination, and business updates where appropriate
  • Send operational SMS messages to active DriverLine delivery drivers for route confirmations, scheduling coordination, dispatch updates, availability checks, and same-day delivery coverage communication
  • Manage driver communications and coverage operations
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who assist with our operations (such as email platforms or CRM systems)
  • Professional advisors (legal, accounting) as needed
  • Government authorities when required by law

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@yourdriverline.com.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience and analyze site traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

SMS Terms & Conditions

About Our SMS Program

DriverLine, operated by ATOR Enterprises Inc., may send SMS messages to active DriverLine delivery drivers for operational purposes only, including route confirmations, scheduling coordination, dispatch updates, availability checks, and same-day delivery coverage communication. DriverLine SMS is not used for customers, prospects, website visitors, marketing leads, promotions, or sales.

For the DriverLine offline onboarding SMS consent workflow and public consent form copy, see https://www.yourdriverline.com/driver-sms-consent.html.

Consent

Active DriverLine drivers provide SMS consent during onboarding or contractor setup before SMS messages are sent. By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you agree to receive operational SMS messages from DriverLine. Message frequency varies based on assigned routes and operational needs. SMS consent is optional and is not required as a condition of employment, contractor engagement, purchase, or service.

Message Types

Active DriverLine drivers may receive messages about:

  • Route confirmations
  • Availability checks
  • Dispatch updates
  • Scheduling coordination
  • Same-day coverage communication
  • Other active-driver operational communications

Opting Out

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, you will receive a confirmation message and no further texts will be sent unless you opt back in.

Help

For assistance, reply HELP to any message, contact our SMS support phone at 720-780-5705, or email info@yourdriverline.com.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. DriverLine does not charge for SMS messages, but your carrier may.

Carriers

SMS services are supported on major U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

SMS opt-in consent and mobile phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our full Privacy Policy above for details on how we handle your information.
